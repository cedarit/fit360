# Fit360

**Learn · train · understand**

Fit360 teaches the science behind fitness and everyday health in plain language, then helps people apply it to their own training, with the reasoning always visible. It is a learning programme, a YouTube teaching channel and, later, a paid app.

## The one-line pitch

Know the *why* behind every do and don't: training, food, supplements, body fat, tests and ageing, explained from the evidence for everyday Indians.

## Why it exists

Fit360 began as one person's need: to train himself properly instead of paying for a personal trainer. Certification turned out to matter less than understanding. Understanding turned out to be much bigger than workouts. It covers nutrition, supplements, body composition, lifestyle disease, preventive health tests, ageing, and how to read research without being fooled by it.

Teaching is the best way to learn, so the learning becomes lessons and videos. The lessons, a workout log, explanations of the reasons and a built-in assistant together become a product others can use for a fee.

**Personal need → learning → teaching → practising → sharing → business.**

## Who it's for

1. **Pankaj and his family**: the first users and the proving ground.
2. **YouTube viewers**: people who want to understand fitness and health, not just follow a plan.
3. **Paying app users**: adults, mainly in India at first, who want structured learning, a workout planner and log, and answers with reasons they can trust.

## What makes it different

- **Reasoning, not rules.** Every recommendation shows its rationale, its evidence and its limits.
- **India-relevant.** Indian body composition, Indian diets and lifestyle-disease patterns, and evidence that applies to Indian populations where it exists.
- **Evidence-honest.** Tells the learner how strong a claim is instead of sounding certain.
- **Safe by design.** Medical literacy, not medical practice. Fit360 helps people understand and ask better questions of their doctor. It never replaces the doctor.

## Pillars

| Pillar | What the user gets |
| --- | --- |
| **Learn** | A structured curriculum in terms, modules and lessons, with videos, quizzes, recall cards and sources |
| **Understand** | Interactive labs and diagrams, evidence cards, and plain-language summaries of research |
| **Train** | A workout planner based on transparent rules, a set-by-set log, an exercise library and recovery check-ins |
| **Track** | Manual meal logging and body measurements |
| **Ask** | A built-in assistant that answers from Fit360's own curriculum and sources, within the safety policy |

## Safety boundary (short version)

Fit360 never diagnoses, interprets a person's own test results, prescribes treatment or medication, provides rehabilitation or gives therapeutic-diet advice. It directs people to qualified clinicians for symptoms, injury, illness, medication and test-result questions. Full rules: [safety and AI policy](product/safety-and-ai-policy.md).
