# Fit360 business model (working draft)

This is a working draft. Items marked **open** are decisions for Pankaj. They are tracked in [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md).

## Audience

- **Primary:** adults in India who want to understand fitness and health, not just follow a plan. Many will first meet Fit360 through YouTube.
- **Later:** English-speaking learners elsewhere. Regional-language versions are an option (**open**).
- **Not at launch:** under-18s, and anyone needing clinical or rehabilitation support.

## Value proposition

Science-first, India-relevant fitness and health education. The reasoning behind every recommendation is visible, and the app brings together learning, training, tracking and questions in one place.

## Channels

1. **YouTube:** free lessons build trust and an audience, and show which topics matter.
2. **Web app:** free tier or beta.
3. **Mobile app:** the paid product.

## Revenue (open)

Options to evaluate:

- **Freemium subscription:** free lessons and a basic log; paid access to full terms, labs, the planner and the assistant.
- **One-time course purchase** per term.
- **YouTube income** (ads, memberships), which supports the business but isn't the business.

Things to weigh:

- App-store fees on in-app purchases compared with web payments (for example through an Indian payment gateway), and whether app-store rules allow each.
- Assistant costs per user, which set a floor on the price.
- Indian price sensitivity: monthly and annual price points.

## Competition (to research)

Workout loggers, fitness-education platforms, supplement-evidence sites and Indian fitness apps. Fit360's position is **explanation plus practice, for Indian bodies and diets**. A proper competitor scan is a Phase 3 task.

## Legal and data obligations

*Not legal advice. Get a lawyer's review before Phase 3 (beta users) and again before Phase 4 (paid launch).*

- **Personal data:** workout, meal, body-measurement and check-in data about other people is personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Expect to need clear notice, consent, purpose limits, security safeguards, deletion on request and breach handling.
- **Children:** the Act requires verifiable parental consent to process children's data. Fit360 is 18+ at launch.
- **Health claims:** consumer-protection and advertising rules apply to health and supplement claims in the app and on YouTube. Stay within the safety policy and cite sources.
- **Company:** decide which legal entity operates Fit360 (**open**).
- **Terms and privacy policy:** required before any non-family user signs up.

## Key metrics (from Phase 3)

- Weekly active learners, and lessons completed
- Workouts logged per active user per week
- Retention in weeks 1, 4 and 12
- Conversion from free to paid (Phase 4)
- Assistant cost per paying user
