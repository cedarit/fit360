# Fit360 curriculum

The curriculum is the source of truth for learning goals, scope, sequencing, safety language and content requirements. The app, the YouTube videos and the AI assistant all draw from it.

### Topics that must be covered somewhere

- The dos and don'ts of common exercises, each with its reason
- Fat loss and muscle gain principles
- Supplements: what the evidence says, safety, and quality and regulation in India
- Belly fat and visceral fat; Asian-Indian body composition and why cut-offs differ
- Lifestyle diseases as literacy topics (what they are, risk factors, prevention evidence), never treatment
- Preventive health tests: what each measures, what guidelines generally recommend, and questions to ask a doctor
- Reading a research report: study types, sample, effect size, relative and absolute risk, conflicts of interest
- Ageing: muscle, bone, balance, and training across the lifespan

## What each term specifies

- Intended learner outcomes and prerequisites
- Modules and core vocabulary
- Safe self-application activities that stay educational and non-clinical
- Evidence-appraisal prompts and source expectations
- Explicit safety boundaries and referral language
- Assessment and recall activities that test understanding, not clinical judgement
- A YouTube mapping: which lessons become videos

## Content guardrails

Curriculum content follows the [safety and AI policy](../product/safety-and-ai-policy.md): medical literacy, not medical practice. When content touches symptoms, injury, medical conditions, medications, test results or therapeutic diets, it directs learners to qualified clinicians.

## Authoring workflow

1. Define learning outcomes and scope in the term file.
2. Find authoritative, current sources, following the evidence policy in [PROJECT_CONTEXT.md](../PROJECT_CONTEXT.md). Record the limits of the evidence, including whether it comes from Indian populations.
3. Draft plain-language material and safe examples. Either AI may draft; neither is the authority.
4. Review for technical accuracy, accessibility and the safety boundary (the other AI, then Pankaj).
5. Pankaj approves; then map the content to product requirements and a video script.
